# Demo POS menu photo credits

The cashier preview uses the following food photographs as **illustrative
category fallbacks** when a restaurant product has no uploaded image. They are
not representations of actual dishes sold by Spice Kitchen. Product uploads
always override the fallbacks. These images are fetched from Unsplash at
runtime and may not display without network access.

- Burger photograph by Eiliv Aceron: https://unsplash.com/photos/burger-with-lettuce-and-tomato-uBigm8w_MpA
- Burger and fries photograph by Amanda Lim: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-sandwich-sitting-on-top-of-a-plate-of-food--FWqmBtcyqo
- Margherita pizza photograph by Thomas Wavid Johns: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-pizza-sitting-on-top-of-a-metal-pan-KIIhnE4GIa0
- Chicken biryani photograph by Steve Tsang: https://unsplash.com/photos/food-photography-of-bowl-of-chicken-biryani-OterGMpkdsM
- Chicken kebabs photograph by Mihir Sabnis: https://unsplash.com/photos/several-skewers-of-chicken-and-vegetables-on-a-grill-FOZbF67e-DE

All photos are linked to their original pages and described there as free to use
under the Unsplash License. Do not redistribute the example photos as product
photos for unrelated dishes. Restaurant owners should upload their actual menu
photographs. If the external image fails, the existing menu emoji is displayed.
